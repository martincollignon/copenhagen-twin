Evidence used

          
Roofer roof/wall surfaces fitted to 2019 DHM point clouds · KK footprints · calibrated 2025 Skråfoto · licensed 2018 and 2022 street references · OSM water boundary.

          
Photo sampling density is not position accuracy; Roofer fits are not tied to survey control. Five hero front walls use photo-traced openings, reveals, cornices and storefront details with inferred dimensions; remaining usable edges keep source photo appearance. Red 11 uses subtle inferred brick coursing. Five hero roofs use a source-sampled clay tile material and photo-traced dormers or chimneys. Red 11 has a localized inferred source-vertex roof correction. Quay profiles and paving appearance are inferred from mapped shoreline and nearby terrain. Nineteen fallback envelopes retain flat inferred roof caps where no accepted Roofer mesh was available.

          
Street references: Jenny Andersson / News Øresund, 2022, CC BY 2.0; Liberaler Humanist, 2018, CC BY-SA 3.0. Source records: sources/street-reference/sources.json. OSM © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
