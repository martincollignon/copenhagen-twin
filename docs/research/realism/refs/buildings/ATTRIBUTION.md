- Original: File:Elmegade No 20-22.jpg
- Author: Leif Jørgensen
- Date taken: 2019-01-28
- File page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Elmegade_No_20-22.jpg
- Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0
- Reference for: A continuous Nørrebro perimeter-block street wall with three adjoining facades: beige render scored as ashlar, pale blue and salmon-red render. Each has a dark-grey rendered ground-floor plinth with shopfronts and an arched entrance door, four upper storeys plus dormers, string courses at every floor and a projecting cornice. Window frames are dark (black/charcoal) on the beige house and white-painted on the blue and red houses. There is a Greek-key frieze band above the beige plinth, and the dormers are clad in dark metal on a dark roof.
