# Copenhagen street-scene visual references

**Area:** Søerne, Nørrebro, Nørre Søgade and Dronning Louises Bro. **Research and URL checks:** 25 October 2026. **Purpose:** a metric asset guide for a real-time twin, not a construction specification or a complete 2026 street inventory.

## Reading the evidence

- **Published** means a dimension, material or designation appears in a cited municipal/manufacturer document. A catalogue option does not establish installation at a particular address.
- **Observed** means visible in the linked, dated photograph. Photographs establish appearance at that date, not current ownership, exact dimensions or concealed light sources.
- **Estimate** means an editable modelling starting point, not a measured local value. All unsourced dimensions below are explicitly estimates; metres are used unless marked otherwise.
- **Unresolved** means the research did not establish the answer. Keep placement and dimensions configurable rather than silently promoting a guess to a standard.

The main distinction is between the upper street/road, the lower lakeside promenade and the bridge. They have different surfaces, lamps and edge details. Choose a scene date before mixing photographs, proposed works and present-day equipment. The linked municipal PDFs are reference material, not a grant to redistribute their photographs. Downloaded Commons photographs have individual attribution and licence records in [visual-refs/README.md](visual-refs/README.md).

## 1. Street and lake-path lighting

### 1a. Københavnermasten, rørmast and overhead suspension

**Appearance and variants.** Separate the support from the lantern. The Copenhagen/Københavner lantern is a rounded bell with a broad circular underside; it can be mounted on an arm or suspended. A **rørmast** is a plain tubular steel support, with a service-door seam near its base; conical poles taper, cylindrical poles do not. A third, visually important local variant is the open **lattice/gittermast**, visible at the bridge approach in the [2020 corner photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dronning_Louises_Bro_-_corner_01.jpg). Model its open diagonal bracing rather than a solid cylinder. **Københavnermast med kugle** is separately named in KK’s November 2025 lighting specification: retain its ball detail and use that document’s illustrated profile; it is not another name for the lattice support. Exact local mast dimensions remain unresolved.

Wire installations need transverse support cables, shallow sag, façade anchors or freestanding supports, a suspension fitting above each lantern and a power lead. The [2015 Nørre Søgade photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:N%C3%B8rre_S%C3%B8gade_02.JPG) shows multiple lanterns across the road and repeated spans along it. This is local visual evidence, not a measured wire-height schedule.

**Materials/colour and dimensions.** Use mottled zinc-grey for unpainted galvanised tubes and a separate painted-metal variant. The November 2025 SAB lists **Københavnermast RAL 7024** and **wire-support gittermast RAL 6006**, differing from the general public-realm manual’s older lattice palette. Date-match the source and existing paint. **Estimates:** tube height 6–8 m; catenary luminaire centre 8 m above road; longitudinal span spacing 25–35 m; lattice support 8–10 m. These are blockout parameters only. Actual anchors, cable sag, lane alignment and clearances need site measurement. There is no verified universal Søerne spacing. **Published, conditional post-2020 requirements:** about **8.5 m clearance** with exposed live copper between fittings; lower arrangements with power along façades instead require **16 m minimum between fittings** for rescue access. These are not measurements of existing streets. [KK SAB Belysning, November 2025, §§15.2.2 and 15.3.9](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-03/SAB_belysning_koebenhavns_kommune_november%202025.docx).

**References (support/placement):** the two dated Commons photographs above; [KK replacement comparison sheet, showing cylindrical/conical and suspended arrangements](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/26a5930d-cdb3-4c08-9745-c20475c8e32d/0a621492-c3f8-4905-8f9f-5f2c3bcca23a-bilag-3.pdf).

### 1b. Lantern models and light colour

**Published product geometry:** Philips Copenhagen **CGS441** has a 462 mm diameter and 363 mm height; smaller **CGS443** is 380 × 308 mm. The original leaflet describes reinforced-polyester housing, cast-aluminium mounting parts and flat tempered glass; standard finish RAL 7035, with other finishes available. Preserve the shallow bell profile and flat optical opening. The leaflet's mounting recommendation is 4–6 m; this is not a measurement of Nørre Søgade's higher suspended installation. [Philips Copenhagen leaflet, pp.1–2](https://archello.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/documents/2018/02/13/copenhagen.1518546821.0235.pdf).

**Where used and variants:** KK's retained-lighting inventory identifies two **CGS441 CDO-ET 70 W** fittings at **Nørre Søgade 25–27**, on the public connection to Charlotte Ammundsens Plads. That establishes a local model, not the identity of every overhead fitting. **BGS441 / “AMT CGS441”: the exact local asset-code mapping remains unresolved**; do not assign either code to a photographed fitting without its asset record. CDO metal-halide must not be relabelled sodium. [KK inventory, Nørre Søgade row](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/95abc2d8-9d9c-48b2-9bd6-0f6e51a80c6e/9533c5c9-5df0-45a0-911b-2531a3e40032-bilag-3_0.pdf).

KK's replacement sheet also documents **Icon Mini Opal LED**, retained/new Copenhagen LED and **ParkView LED** park fittings. Use these as alternate asset families only where a local photo or asset record identifies them. Citelum's involvement in the lake project below does not prove one citywide luminaire model. [KK replacement comparison](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/26a5930d-cdb3-4c08-9745-c20475c8e32d/0a621492-c3f8-4905-8f9f-5f2c3bcca23a-bilag-3.pdf).

**Night rendering:** current SAB guidance is **3000 K across road classes and urban paths**; it lists Copenhagen mounting at **5–9 m**, normally **RAL 7024**, with **RAL 8011 brown** permitted in central Indre By (source in §1a). The earlier municipal masterplan’s **3000/3500/4000 K** hierarchy is historical, not the current default. For a confirmed historical high-pressure-sodium fixture, an **estimated 2000 K amber** emission is a useful rendering proxy; retain separate mercury/metal-halide/LED variants. Housing age alone cannot determine the spectrum. [KK lighting masterplan, “Lysstemning i København” diagram](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/1df72528-8050-4940-8bac-c8a6433ddde3/5cd8df06-b399-40aa-9f45-40c68597d7a5-bilag-2.pdf).

### 1c. Søerne lower-path lamps

The 2014 municipal project approved roughly **60 low, downward-lighting LED bollards** on the east, west and south sides of **Peblinge Sø**, with midnight dimming; the north side relied on existing road light. Citelum participated in delivery. This is historical project coverage, not confirmation of every lamp around all five lake basins today. [KK approval, 21 August 2014](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Borgerrepr%C3%A6sentationen/m%C3%B8de-21082014/referat/punkt-22).

The accompanying precedent image names **CitySwan** and shows an installation on Refshalevej. Its short dark post and small projecting head are a useful starting silhouette, with light emitted beneath rather than an omnidirectional globe. Distinguish precedent/design intent from an as-built survey. [KK CitySwan illustration](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/ee79d8e3-bb48-4c71-8779-84e859c070cf/31d93e0a-81a6-4dc6-ac8f-fad4900f441f-bilag-2.pdf).

The manufacturer's **BGP444 CitySwan** specification gives black finish, **3000 K**, and curved polycarbonate or flat-glass cover variants. **Estimated blockout:** height 0.9–1.1 m, repeat distance 15 m; neither is a verified Søerne dimension. Exact installed finish/RAL, height and spacing remain unresolved. [Philips Parks & Plazas brochure, CitySwan spread, pp.20–21](https://www.assets.signify.com/is/content/Signify/Assets/philips-lighting/denmark/20210205-parks-plazas-brochure.pdf).

## 2. Litter bins and recycling containers

**KK families:** Pizzakurv is the newer standard: perforated steel box, four rectangular openings, **240 L**. Older Københavnerkurv: cylindrical stainless-steel shell, solid/perforated versions, city-arms cut-out, **60 L**. Tårnkurv: square, **140 L**. Park variants include green/black finishes; old glass containers have ribbed bodies. [KK inventory manual, pp.6–9](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-04/retningslinjer-for-brug-af-inventar-i-byens-rum-december-2020.pdf).

For a dimensioned cylindrical comparator, HITSA's **Copenhagen** bin is 60 L, with **678 mm top height**, 2 mm lid/shell and 4 mm plinth, stainless or powder-coated stainless exterior and a black plastic liner. It has an optional ashtray. **This catalogue product is not proven identical to every KK Københavnerkurv.** [HITSA Copenhagen datasheet](https://hitsa.dk/files/_OPEN/Skraldespande/Copenhagen/Copenhagen-skraldespand-datablad-dk.pdf).

**Where/colour:** place individual bins from photos at benches, busy corners and stops; do not distribute uniformly. KK palette colours are summarised in §10: grey RAL 7021, park green RAL 6009/black RAL 9005, transport blue RAL 5011 where applicable. Bare stainless remains a separate material. Decals, liners, openings and hinges matter more at street scale than dense perforation geometry; use a masked texture for distant assets. **Estimated envelopes:** Pizzakurv 0.65 × 0.65 × 1.1 m; old glass cube 1.3 × 1.3 × 1.6 m. These are unmeasured proxies; glass-container capacity and exact installed colour are unresolved.

**Modern sorting points** differ from old glass cubes. KK’s 2020 C.F. Møller design sheet presents three related forms: above-ground cube, wheeled-bin enclosure and inlet for a buried container. This is design documentation, not proof of installation at a specific address. Use dark rounded/chamfered shells, front intake flaps and fraction labels, matching the photographed form. Exact RAL, installed capacities and dimensions were not established. Their context is residential street edges; presence on the lower lake paths is not confirmed. [KK sorting-point design sheet, C.F. Møller, 2020](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/fe03ea71-01dc-45cf-9d94-67725b39d44f/65f5f0a1-26d6-4e80-a738-95f2108425fa-bilag-5.pdf).

**Future variant:** a 2027 budget proposal describes an **80 L steel Mini Tårnkurv**, H 1300 × W 580 × D 520 mm, opening at 1050 mm. This is useful asset documentation but **not evidence of completed 2026 rollout**. [KK efficiency catalogue, Mini Tårnkurv illustration, PDF p.12](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/215325b3-dbc6-452c-ab53-ec1c53ebd915/3df37d10-2e54-402f-b56e-5235bae5d28c-bilag-2.pdf).

## 3. Bollards

KK lists **SP11–13** galvanized/grey/green bollards, reflector/removable variants and site-specific granite. Its **1.6 m maximum anti-car gap** is not a placement grid; preserve accessible passage. [KK inventory manual, pp.12–13](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-04/retningslinjer-for-brug-af-inventar-i-byens-rum-december-2020.pdf).

**Geometry/material:** HITSA’s **LENTA U** comparator is cylindrical, galvanized, with a rounded top and no reflector groove: **Ø89/114 mm**, **800 mm above ground**. Powder coating and removable versions are available. It is a dimensioned comparator, not proof of the exact SP model at Søerne. Keep mottled matte zinc distinct from painted dark-green metal; KK's green palette reference is RAL 6009, but exact local coatings require a photo match. [HITSA LENTA U product specification](https://hitsa.dk/produkter/lenta-u-pullert/); [KK colour guidance, p.8](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-04/retningslinjer-for-indretning-af-byens-rum.pdf).

**Use/variants:** vehicle-entry controls, pedestrian corners and parking exclusion; include removable sockets only where visible. A granite proxy can be a grey/pink crystalline block with a softened or chamfered top, **estimated 0.25 × 0.25 × 0.8 m**. Its precise shape and locations around Søerne remain unresolved. Do not confuse stone bridge/chain-fence posts with standalone traffic bollards, or the CitySwan lighting bollard with a vehicle barrier.

## 4. Benches, including lake-path seating

The **Københavnerbænk** is KK's characteristic urban/inner-park bench, with double and circular variants. Århusbænk and timber Skovbænk are other park families. [KK inventory manual, pp.2–4](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-04/retningslinjer-for-brug-af-inventar-i-byens-rum-december-2020.pdf).

**Appearance:** ornate black cast-iron end frames, curved armrests and broad green-painted wooden seat/back boards. HITSA publishes **1800 or 2169 mm lengths**, seat planks **180 × 35 mm**, back planks **145 × 28 mm**, mahogany timber and traditional **RAL 6009** green, alongside oiled/raw options. Use the selected product drawing for board count and frame profile. [HITSA Københavnerbænk](https://hitsa.dk/produkter/kobenhavnerbaenk/).

A second supplier's comparable bench is **L1800 × D650 × H900 mm**, with **RAL 9005** black cast iron and optional RAL 6009 timber. This is a dimensioned comparator, not proof of the exact supplier at the Lakes. Seat height **0.45 m is an estimate** until the selected drawing/site is measured. [Urban Elements Københavnerbænk](https://urban-elements.dk/vare/urban-koebenhavnerbaenk/).

**Local use:** the [2024 bridge-lamp photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gadelampe_p%C3%A5_Dronning_Louises_Bro_01.jpg) shows green boards and dark decorative bench ends against the parapet. Lower-path benches should follow local photos, usually taking advantage of water views; spacing is irregular and **unverified**. The bridge approach's long curved bench runs (§8) need their own geometry. Weathering should expose edges and board grain without making every bench equally distressed.

## 5. Bicycle racks and parked bicycles

**KK standard rack:** galvanised wheel-holder modules, **2.5/3 m**, five/six single-sided or ten/twelve double-sided spaces; 90°/45°/30° arrangements. Mounting posts are **0.6 m**, with a 0.8 m alternative. [KK inventory manual, p.15](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-04/retningslinjer-for-brug-af-inventar-i-byens-rum-december-2020.pdf).

For close-up detail, HITSA **HH20** provides **Ø35 mm rails, Ø14 mm tapered loop rods, Ø89 mm posts, 500 mm wheel centres** (600 optional), and loop centre **350 mm above ground**. It is a comparable product, **not confirmed as the precise KK standard model**. Make galvanized finish softly mottled; offer powder-coated variants only where photographed. [HITSA HH20 datasheet](https://hitsa.dk/files/_OPEN/Cykelstativer/Hh20/Hh20-cykelstativ-datablad-dk.pdf).

**Placement:** keep modules at pavement edges or designated parking fields with circulation behind them, not across the entire footway. Leave separate space for cargo bikes. Use the municipal layout drawings for arrangement and access rather than inferring that nominal rack pitch equals occupied handlebar width. [KK cycle-parking guidance, 2017](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-04/retningslinjer-for-etablering-af-cykelparkering%202017.pdf).

**Bicycle mix:** local photographs in the image pack show upright city bikes, step-through and diamond frames, baskets, carriers, mudguards, locks and mixed black/metal/coloured frames. **Estimated ordinary-bike envelope:** L1.75–1.9 × handlebar W0.55–0.7 m. Vary lean, steering angle and accessories; do not claim a measured statistical mix. Two-wheel long-john and three-wheel box cargo bikes are separate silhouettes.

For a dimensioned cargo asset, **Christiania Classic Medium** is **2080 × 870 × 1170 mm**; Short/Long lengths are **1870/2380 mm**. Its box sits between two front wheels, with a single rear rider wheel; published construction includes **9 mm birch plywood**, galvanized profiles and an aluminium frame. Match canopy, box colour and front shape to the chosen variant; no universal RAL was found. [Christiania Bikes Classic](https://www.christianiabikes.com/en/classic/).

## 6. Bus shelters, Movia poles and countdown displays

**AFA/JCDecaux Copenhagen family:** Holscher's second-generation design has a slender roof/drainage system, clear glass enclosure, wooden bench and configurable digital information. The design photographs show a dark-blue frame, projecting flat roof and optional advertising side box. Earlier curved-roof shelters are a separate historical variant; date and identify the target stop before choosing. [Holscher, AFA JCDecaux Copenhagen](https://holscherdesign.com/cases/afa-jcdecaux-copenhagen/).

KK's manual describes galvanized steel, tempered glass and blue identity finish; narrow model **1.2 × 3.85 m**, advertisement-free Direct **1.2 × 2.62 m**. [KK inventory manual, pp.10–11](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-04/retningslinjer-for-brug-af-inventar-i-byens-rum-december-2020.pdf).

**Published drawing variants:** Model 1 roof projection **1.943 m**, roof height **2.649 m**, overall stop pole **3.87 m**; Model 2 projection **1.236 m**, upper frame **2.488 m**, pole **3.901 m**. These dimension arrows refer to different elements: do not combine them into one invented shelter. The same drawings give minimum islands of **2.95/2.25 m** respectively. Use the elevations for the roof, glazing bands, bench, ad panel and foundations. [KK dimensioned shelter/foundation drawings, PDF pp.1 and 5](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-04/M%C3%A5lfaste%20tegninger%20for%20busl%C3%A6sk%C3%A6rme%20og%20fundamenter..pdf).

**Stop markers/displays:** Movia uses modular Holscher posts in blue, green or grey, plus a simpler cylindrical rørstander. Countdown can attach to either; it is not universal. In the Copenhagen drawings the narrow rectangular post has a yellow bus pictogram header, route panels and timetable inserts. Keep the small dark countdown box with amber/yellow text distinct from a larger integrated digital information screen. **Estimated post width:** 0.25–0.35 m; exact width unresolved. Transport blue **RAL 5011** is the KK palette reference (§10), not proof that every Movia installation uses that coating. [Movia stops and equipment](https://moviatrafik.dk/kommune-region/radgivning/stoppesteder-og-udstyr).

**Where:** map actual bus stops and boarding islands on the chosen street/date. A shelter is not implied merely by a bus route; ad-free, narrow and pole-only stops need separate assets. No fixed spacing or complete local 2026 shelter inventory was established.

## 7. Parking bays, P-automater and zone signs

**Appearance/variants:** white painted or thermoplastic boundaries sit flush on dark asphalt; some bays instead use contrasting paving. Use scuffed edges, patch repairs and occasional interrupted lines. **E33** is the white P on blue; **E68/E69** identify zone entry/end. Copy the photographed supplementary restrictions and time panels. Municipal parking-map colours are not pavement colours. [Illustrated Danish road-marking regulation, E33/E68/E69 and T33](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2021/2511/pdf).

**Dimensions:** a June 2025 Øster Søgade project records existing **2.0 m** bay widths and proposes **2.3 m**. This is a local, dated comparison, not proof that the proposal was built. **Estimated blockout only:** parallel W2.0–2.2 × L5–6 m; perpendicular W2.4–2.5 × L5 m; white line width 0.1 m. These are not measured Nørrebro bays or a statement of current minimum legal dimensions. [KK Øster Søgade pre-analysis decision, parking section](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Teknik-%20og%20Milj%C3%B8udvalget/m%C3%B8de-16062025/referat/punkt-17).

**P-automat:** upright metal cabinet with inset display/card interface, instructions and P identifier. Use a blue-painted variant based on the KK **RAL 5011** palette (§10), but confirm the actual cabinet against a local photograph. **Estimated envelope:** H1.5–1.8 × W0.35–0.5 × D0.25–0.4 m. Exact installed maker/model, current finish and spacing remain unresolved; do not scatter machines procedurally. Zone signs are flat rectangular panels on poles; **estimated panel envelope 0.6 × 0.9 m**, with dimensions/text varying by sign assembly.

A March 2026 municipal investment proposal reports around **450 machines remaining** after earlier removals and proposes removal of the remainder in **2027**. Former sites may carry information signs. Treat this as a proposal/status report, **not evidence that every machine has disappeared**. [KK investment proposals, BC25](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/907ed326-fed7-4530-b223-fc002e44f8a2/a07b5c17-1969-4ce3-99a0-01b0cf92db59-bilag-3.pdf).

## 8. Søerne edges, rescue equipment and Dronning Louises Bro

### 8a. Lake edge and paths

**Appearance/material:** Peblinge/Sortedams edges include fitted granite blocks with visible joints, staining and a darker wet/algal waterline. Their construction and repair details vary by stretch; the 2024 engineering report distinguishes granite-block repairs from other concrete-edge works. Do not model every basin with the same continuous wall section. It documents localized temporary barriers and repairs; those are dated conditions, not permanent railings. [KK/Artelia lake-edge repair application, especially pp.1–5 and 32–34](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/af8eddb2-1392-49c9-a6a5-8449c9058094/326c3ed6-7ee7-40a3-8be3-9e1e58507b16-bilag-2_1.pdf).

**Path surfaces/levels:** distinguish compacted pale beige/grey fine gravel on lower walking paths from asphalt on roads/cycle tracks and slab/cobble footways above. Municipal records describe gravel paths, grass slopes, stairs and ramps around the Lakes. [KK lakeside reinstatement description](https://www.kk.dk/dagsordener-og-referater/Bygge-%20og%20Teknikudvalget/m%C3%B8de-26032003/dagsorden/punkt-3).

The [2015 Nørre Søgade view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:N%C3%B8rre_S%C3%B8gade_01.JPG) visibly separates the high street footway from the lower path and shows low path lights. The [Peblinge façade view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peblinge_Dossering_facades.jpg) shows an open water edge. **Do not add a continuous waist-high fence around the Lakes.** Stair handrails, bridge parapets, chains around planted areas and temporary barriers are separate localized elements. A photo cannot establish an unseen stretch's railing status.

**Estimated blockout:** lower path width 3–5 m; visible edge drop to water 0.3–0.8 m; coping/block scale 0.3–0.6 m. These ranges are provisional, vary with water level and are not surveyed dimensions. Model the actual crest, water level and slope from terrain/engineering data before committing geometry. Use roughness and staining masks rather than uniform black seams or exaggerated displacement.

### 8b. Redningsposter / life-ring posts

TrygFonden documents its **2012 cooperation with KK to place rescue rings at Søerne**, including use at Sankt Jørgens Sø. This proves the equipment family belongs in the scene, but not the present location/count of every post. [TrygFonden 2012 season report, rescue-ring spread](https://trygfonden.dk/-/media/files/pdf/publikationer/sikkerhed/kystlivredning-saesonrapport-2012.pdf).

**Appearance:** ring, surrounding grab rope, coiled throwing line and mounting bracket on a slim post, with instruction/identification graphics. Match the red/orange ring and contrasting markings to the dated product/site photograph; no exact local RAL was established. TrygFonden offers a **2 m post**, either surface-mounted or joined to a **1 m burial spike**. These are product dimensions, not surveyed installed exposed heights. [TrygFonden rescue-ring/post photographs and specification](https://respektforvand.dk/udstyr-og-taarne/redningskranse/ansog-redningskrans).

For a geometry comparator, Engholm's Taurus ring is **750 mm outside / 450 mm inside diameter**, polyethylene exterior, polyurethane core and four reflective strips. **It is not confirmed as the exact TrygFonden/Søerne ring model.** [Engholm Taurus](https://www.engholm.dk/redningskrans-taurus). Preserve the visible bracket, rope and sign; omit unseen core geometry. Exact local spacing is unresolved: position only from a map/photo/site survey, rather than importing marina spacing rules.

### 8c. Dronning Louises Bro

**Published structure:** a granite embankment/bridge with **three arched water openings**, four rounded stone-set approaches, ornamental railings, four lamps on each parapet, four end flagpoles and curved bench runs. The heritage description records asphalt roadway and slab/cobble sidewalks, with decorative lamp shafts and civic arms. [Heritage case report hosted by KK, pp.1–2](https://kk.sites.itera.dk/apps/kk_monuments/pdf/190_db43_1.pdf).

**Observed appearance:** the [2013 side view](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dronning_Louises_Bro_01.JPG) shows large warm grey/tan masonry courses, projecting horizontal mouldings, dark staining below and substantial stone parapets. In the [2024 lamp close-up](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gadelampe_p%C3%A5_Dronning_Louises_Bro_01.jpg), the ornate shaft is brown, the globe cage has green patina and the glazing is dark/transparent. These are photo observations, **not verified RAL specifications**. Do not substitute a standard Copenhagen bell lamp for this heritage fitting. Keep roadway wire lamps as separate assets (§1).

**Width:** KK's 2017–2025 cycle-priority plan labels the bridge cycle track **3.9 m wide**. That is a dated published track width, not the bridge's total width. **Estimated blockout total deck width: about 25 m**, pending a surveyed cross-section; do not present it as a verified modern dimension. Sidewalk, cycle-track and bus-lane proportions must be measured independently. **Estimated heritage lamp height:** 3–4 m above its stone base; exact dimension unresolved. [KK cycle-priority plan draft, printed p.19](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/0831979e-c478-41e0-a867-4d56b79b000e/183bdb82-f823-4a0a-a2a4-03339071d65f-bilag-1.pdf).

## 9. Trees and tree grates

**Species/use:** create separate **Tilia** (lime/linden) and **Aesculus** (horse-chestnut) families. KK's April 2026 inventory records **5,325 street limes, 26% of the recorded city total**. This supports a citywide Tilia asset, not automatic placement on every lakeside tree point. [KK tree inventory response, April 2026](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-04/16.04.26%20-%20svar%20til%20Kristine%20Ammitzb%C3%B8ll-Bille%20%28B%29%20om%20Tr%C3%A6politik%20og%20-bestemmelser.pdf).

The Øster Søgade pre-analysis documents chestnut planting at the northeast end of the Lakes, including the Østerbrogade terrace rows. It contains useful contemporary site photographs; later proposed layouts must not be read as completed work. **Exact Nørre Søgade species/ages remain unresolved without a local tree inventory.** [KK/LINQ Øster Søgade pre-analysis, planting pp.15–18](https://indrebylokaludvalg.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2025-04/I100373_C05_Foranalyse.pdf).

**Modelling appearance:** Tilia has fine heart-shaped leaves and a dense oval/rounded crown; horse chestnut has larger palmate leaf clusters, heavier spreading branches and spring flower candles. Give both grey-brown bark and seasonal foliage; chestnut browning should not become a universal summer treatment. **Estimated mature assets:** H12–22 m, crown Ø8–15 m, trunk Ø0.35–0.9 m. Use irregular crowns, young replacements and leafless variants. Positions and spacing should follow imagery/tree points rather than a repeated grid; published local spacing was not established.

**Grates:** use flush round/square dark metal grilles only where trees emerge through paving. Trees in lakeside soil/grass do not automatically require a grate. Milford **Sahara**, a comparator rather than a confirmed Søerne product, is **1195 × 1195 mm cast iron** or **1200 × 1200 mm steel**, with **Ø600 mm opening**. Uncoated steel can weather rust-red; cast iron reads darker. The product was phased out in 2023, so it is a historical geometry reference, not a current procurement recommendation. Exact installed Københavnerrist geometry remains unresolved. [Milford Sahara](https://www.milford.dk/produkter/sahara-traehulsrist).

## 10. Street-name signs, traffic signs/signals and cycle-track kerbs

### 10a. KK blue signs and shared furniture palette

Street-name signs are thin horizontal dark-blue plaques with white lettering, often attached to corner façades. Generations differ in border, type and fixings. Preserve the photographed spelling and line breaks. **Estimated plaque:** W0.7–1.2 × H0.18–0.25 m; mounting height and enamel/sheet construction remain site-specific and unverified. [Commons street-sign reference gallery](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Street_signs_in_Copenhagen).

KK's public-realm colour guidance publishes **RAL 5011** for street-name signs and transport/advertising/P-automat equipment; **RAL 6009** for green bench timber; **RAL 9005** black; **RAL 7021 Pearl** grey; handrails **RAL 9011**. Railing guards have separate **RAL 7040/7035** options. These are policy references, not proof that weathered objects or all historical stock match. Its lattice-mast RAL 7024 guidance differs from the newer lighting-specific SAB (§1); retain the source/date distinction. **No official sRGB hex equivalence was verified.** Do not silently replace these with a retail “Københavnerblå” paint or an arbitrary web RAL-to-hex conversion. [KK public-realm arrangement guide, printed p.8](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/2026-04/retningslinjer-for-indretning-af-byens-rum.pdf).

### 10b. Danish traffic signs and signal heads

**Signs:** use the Danish artwork: red-bordered white warning/prohibition signs, blue mandatory/information faces and separate supplementary panels, on slim grey poles. Reflective-looking faces should respond to headlights rather than emit light. Shapes, arrows and glyphs belong in a decal/atlas; metal backs and pole brackets remain geometry. **Estimated common sign-face width:** 0.5–0.7 m; actual sign sizes and mounting heights vary. [Danish illustrated road-marking regulation](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2021/2511/pdf).

**Signals:** dark housings, short hoods and vertically stacked red–amber–green circular apertures. The verified 2023 regulation specifies main/pedestrian/arrow openings **Ø200 mm ±10%**, bicycle openings **Ø100 mm ±10%**, and aperture gaps no greater than half their diameter. The bicycle unit has an illuminated **white bicycle on blue above**: either **80 × 130 mm** or **Ø100 mm**, ±10%. This upper identifier is a useful Danish detail. Exact local head manufacturer, body colour and pole height were not identified; **estimated pole/head top 2.5–3 m** is only a blockout. [Signal geometry regulation, 2023, §§250–251](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2023/426/pdf). These editions are cited for visual/geometry reference, not as a certification of the current consolidated road rules.

### 10c. Cycle-track section

Model **road → raised asphalt cycle track → raised slab/cobble footway**, with pale stone kerbs at both level changes. KK's **Cykelfokus 2024 draft** gives upstands **80–120 mm road-to-track** and **60–80 mm track-to-footway**. These are draft design values, not measurements of existing Nørre Søgade kerbs. [Cykelfokus 2024 draft, p.6](https://www.kk.dk/sites/default/files/agenda/03e7571c-7870-4344-95bf-6d33c5779242/81e1e2f8-dd62-415e-be0c-ea31526f3f0b-bilag-2.pdf).

Kommuneplan 2024 width guidance spans **2.5–4.0 m** by bicycle flow, with **PLUSnet minimum 2.8 m**. Existing widths vary; use the bridge's separately documented width (§8) where relevant. Include lowered crossings, drainage slots and worn kerb edges. Blue crossing patches are intersection treatments, not the default colour for an entire cycle track. [KK cycle-infrastructure standards](https://kp24.kk.dk/retningslinjer/trafik/cykelinfrastruktur/standard-for-cykelinfrastrukturen).

## 11. Façade palette and Nørrebro karré

**Massing/use:** create continuous street walls around courtyards, with distinct plots, repeated vertical window bays, gateways, pitched roofs, cornices and occasional taller corner features. Nørrebro side streets are not interchangeable with the more monumental lake-facing façades and Søtorvet. The municipal atlas is the neighbourhood-scale reference. [KK Bydelsatlas Nørrebro](https://kk.sites.itera.dk/apps/kk_pub2/pdf/1330_EysnkhaOIn.pdf).

**Material families:** ochre plaster, exposed **gule mursten** (yellow brick), red brick, off-white/grey plaster, and mixed brick/stone-like trim. Historicist fronts may combine exposed brick above with rusticated/rendered ground floors and cast ornament. Separate plinth, cornice, window recess, roof tile/slate and timber/metal windows; do not flatten the entire elevation into one coloured plane. [Slots- og Kulturstyrelsen, town-house architectural history 1480–1930](https://slks.dk/fileadmin/user_upload/kulturarv/publikationer/emneopdelt/bygninger/Bygningsbevaring/1.3%20Byhusenes%20arkitekturhistorie%201480-1930.pdf).

The [Peblinge Dossering façade photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peblinge_Dossering_facades.jpg) shows red brick, yellow brick, pale plaster, contrasting trim and both red-tile/dark roofs side by side. [Nørre Søgade 25](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:N%C3%B8rre_S%C3%B8gade_25.jpg) supplies a local corner example: red brick, pale rustication, arched details, dark window frames and a strongly projecting cornice. These dated photos are useful colour relationships, not calibrated albedo samples.

**Scale/variants:** **estimated blockout 4–6 storeys, cornice H15–23 m, window-bay pitch 2.5–3.5 m**. Derive final heights and footprint from each building, including attic storeys, dormers, chimney stacks and party-wall steps. Courtyard fronts may be plainer than street fronts. Keep render chalky/rough, mortar quieter than brick, and hue variation modest within each façade. **No universal official RAL/hex façade palette was established**: retain ochre/yellow/red/off-white material families and calibrate per building under neutral lighting.
